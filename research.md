My interests are in partial differential equations, functional
analysis, and variational methods, with a focus on nonlinear PDEs.

## Current interests

My work so far has centred around elliptic partial differential
equations. During my BS–MS thesis, I studied Sobolev spaces and
weak solutions of linear elliptic PDEs. Since then, I have been
trying to understand the nonlinear side of the subject.

My current focus is on nonlinear elliptic equations and
variational methods. I am interested in questions concerning
the existence, regularity, and qualitative behaviour of
solutions.

I am also studying nonvariational methods for nonlinear
elliptic PDEs, including monotonicity methods, fixed-point
methods, and sub- and supersolution techniques.

## Background

During my BS–MS thesis, I studied Sobolev spaces and their
applications to linear elliptic partial differential
equations, with a focus on weak solutions and regularity.

I am now building on this background through independent
study of calculus of variations and nonlinear elliptic PDEs,
primarily using L. C. Evans' *Partial Differential Equations*.

## Expository notes

Alongside my study, I am developing a set of expository notes on
elliptic partial differential equations. They are written as a
companion to my study of the subject, with an emphasis on filling
