About

Hi, I’m Shreeram Jaikisan. This site is my space for exploring elliptic partial differential equations and trying to understand the connection between linear and nonlinear elliptic PDEs. My main motivation is to solidify my own understanding by writing down these ideas and trying to explain them in my own words.

What This Site Is

These notes are part of an ongoing learning process. I explore topics that interest me, work through them in detail, and write down the parts that I find useful or difficult. A lot of what I write here comes from questions I had while studying, and from trying to fill in the steps or explanations that I felt were missing.

While I try to keep the notes clear and accurate, they are still a reflection of my understanding at the time of writing. As I learn more, some explanations may change, some details may turn out to be incomplete, and there may be mistakes.

Why I’m Sharing Them

During my MS thesis, I came across many notes, explanations, and write-ups by other people that helped me immensely. Some of them made ideas much clearer to me than they were when I first encountered them in textbooks. I am very grateful to the people who took the time to write those things down and share them.

This site is, in some sense, my attempt to do the same. I hope that some of these explorations might be useful to someone else studying elliptic PDEs, or simply trying to understand these ideas a little better.

About the Notes

The notes are based primarily on L. C. Evans’ Partial Differential Equations. They are not intended to replace a standard textbook. They are companion notes from my own study, where I try to fill in omitted steps in proofs, explain ideas that I found difficult on a first reading, add illustrations where they help, and include solutions to selected exercises.

I will continue to revise and expand them as my understanding develops and as I come across different explanations and perspectives in other books and research papers.
