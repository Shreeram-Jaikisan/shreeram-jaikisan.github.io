RESEARCH

Research

My interests are in partial differential equations, functional analysis, and variational methods, with a focus on nonlinear elliptic PDEs.

Areas of interest

I am interested in using ideas from functional analysis and the calculus of variations to study partial differential equations. I am also interested in the analytical properties of their solutions.

  • Partial differential equations
  • Functional analysis
  • Sobolev spaces
  • Nonlinear elliptic PDEs
  • Calculus of variations
  • Critical point theory

Current interests

My current focus is on nonlinear elliptic equations and variational methods. I am interested in questions concerning the existence, regularity, and qualitative behaviour of solutions.

I am also studying nonvariational methods for nonlinear elliptic PDEs, including monotonicity methods, fixed-point methods, and sub- and supersolution techniques.

Background

During my BS–MS thesis, I studied Sobolev spaces and their applications to linear elliptic partial differential equations, with a focus on weak solutions and regularity.

I am now building on this background through independent study of calculus of variations and nonlinear elliptic PDEs, primarily using L. C. Evans' Partial Differential Equations.

Expository notes

Alongside my study, I am developing a set of expository notes on elliptic partial differential equations. They are written as a companion to my study of the subject, with an emphasis on filling in details of proofs and explaining ideas that I found difficult on a first reading.
